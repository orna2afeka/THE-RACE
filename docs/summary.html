About these figures

Everything here comes from the team's own telemetry: 138,184 samples sent from the car during the race. It is not the official result. Laps, positions and classification are the organiser's to publish, and their timing is the one that counts.

Distance is the car's odometer, which reads about 1% long; 196 laps of the 4.0 km circuit is 784 km. Energy is net of regeneration, measured at the motor controller. Record laps are taken only from laps the crew cut by hand on a full circuit, so a short lap cannot claim the fastest time. The hour-by-hour chart shows the 193 laps the pit holds a full record of; the car's lap counter, corrected by the crew during the race, finished on 196.
